Nov 26-28 2025

Field Trip with NIEER Delegation in Australia

Hosted a group from 中国科学院西北生态环境资源研究院 (Northwest Institute of Eco-Environment and Resources, NIEER) and conducted a field trip to several sites in Australia.

Sites visited: Rubicon Water, Goulburn-Murray Water, Goulburn-Broken Catchment Authority, Yarrawonga Weir, and Hume Dam. We stayed at Shepparton and Albury - very beautiful cities!

NIEER delegation: Prof. Xufeng Wang (王旭峰), A/Prof. Chao Mu (穆超), Dr. Peiyuan Chen (陈沛源), Xiaoying Liu (刘晓颖), Yixuan Wang (王艺璇), Jiangzhe Han (韩江哲), and Fa Du (杜发).

UoM participants: Prof. QJ Wang, Dr. Yuhang Zhang, and Tianyan Zhang.
